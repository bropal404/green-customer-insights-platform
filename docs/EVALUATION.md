# Evaluation report

Every model's metrics on the **validation set**, with the test set and a simple baseline next to them. The tables
below are generated by the pipeline's `evaluate` stage (`src/gcip/models/evaluate.py`) from the 30,000-customer run and
copied unchanged from `demo/pipeline/metrics/evaluation_report.md`; the same numbers are in
`demo/pipeline/metrics/evaluation_report.json`. Test set predictions: `demo/pipeline/predictions/test_predictions.csv`.

## Method

**Data splits.** Customers are split once, seeded, 70% train / 15% validation / 15% test (21,062 / 4,393 / 4,545
customers), and every model uses the same split. Validation chooses hyperparameters (LightGBM random search, scored by
average precision) and the categoriser's ensemble weights; test is used only for the final numbers. The categoriser also
holds out 20% of merchants, which never appear in training, so accuracy on them measures generalisation to new merchants.

**Labels.** A take-up is a product opened between 1 April and 30 September 2026 (from the product-holdings extract),
for a customer eligible for it at the 31 March 2026 snapshot. The simulator decides take-up from each customer's hidden
situation; the model never sees it.

**No leakage.** Features and the Customer 360 are rebuilt using only data up to 31 March 2026 (point in time),
labels come from the later window, splits are by customer (no customer is in two splits), the calibrator is fitted out
of fold on train, the suggestion threshold comes from the train take-up rate, and the simulator's truth vault is used
only to evaluate (oracle ceiling, truth recovery), never as an input. The 30 September 2026 snapshot is used only for
live scoring.

**Baselines.** FR3: a rules score (share of the product's reason signals that are on), a logistic regression (in
`propensity_report.json`) and an oracle ceiling (the simulator's exact probability). FR2: the rules alone. Targeting:
the rules and random choice, contacting the same number of customers.

**Metrics.** ROC-AUC and PR-AUC (ranking; PR-AUC suits rare take-ups), Brier score and expected calibration error
(whether a propensity of 0.2 means about 1 in 5), lift in the top 10%, and at the suggestion threshold the share
selected, precision (take-up rate of those selected) and recall. Validation and test agree closely, so the model was not
tuned to one split.

## Scenario tests

| Scenario (brief) | What the system does | Where it is tested or measured |
|---|---|---|
| Missing or conflicting data | FR1 audit (control totals, fingerprints, 36 validation checks) stops or repairs a load; missing behaviour lowers data coverage and so confidence; 163 suggestions withheld for low confidence | `tests/test_ingest.py` (injected issues, tampered extract, one-penny change); `demo/data/meta/data_validation.json` |
| Products that should not be recommended | Eligibility rules block 103,095 customer x product pairs; 21,498 have no marketing consent; memory stops a product the customer already has and pauses after "not interested" or 3 ignored contacts (667); credit offers to vulnerable customers go to human review (291) | `demo/pipeline/recommendations/decisions.parquet` (one audited decision per customer x product); `tests/test_impact_memory.py` (cool-downs, fatigue) |
| Insufficient impact data | No estimate is made without the inputs; 644 suggestions withheld as "impact cannot be estimated", 15 as "impact too small"; every estimate carries a PCAF data-quality score | `tests/test_impact_memory.py::test_no_number_without_the_inputs`; FR4 truth recovery below |
| Changed customer circumstances | Suggestions are rebuilt from the live snapshot (30 Sep 2026), not the training one; newer responses override older ones; "already have it" stops the product | `tests/test_impact_memory.py::test_memory_events_override_older_responses`; memory replay (`metrics/memory_replay.json`) |

`scripts/run_scenario_tests.py` runs these scenarios end to end on the saved models in about 10 seconds. Each one changes one thing about real customers (vehicle record lost, no vehicle at all, a recent EV, no energy data, a vulnerability flag, financial difficulty, consent withdrawn, product now held) or injects 5% nulls into the transaction feed, reruns eligibility, scoring, impact and suggestions, and checks the decision.

Rerun everything, including this report, on 2,000 customers with `./run.sh --quick` (about 3-5 minutes), or on 30,000
with `./run.sh --rebuild`.
